# JN0-364-Exam-Questions-Help-You-Pass-Service-Provider-Routing-and-Switching---Specialist-Exam
The JN0-364 Service Provider Routing and Switching - Specialist (JNCIS-SP) exam is designed for networking professionals who want to validate their knowledge of routing and switching technologies in Junos OS environments. 
As the written exam for the JNCIS-SP certification, JN0-364 focuses on essential service provider networking concepts, platform configuration, and troubleshooting skills.

For candidates preparing for the exam, practicing with relevant [JN0-364 exam questions](https://www.certqueen.com/JN0-364.html) is an effective way to become familiar with the exam objectives, identify knowledge gaps, and strengthen confidence before test day.

## JN0-364 Exam Overview

Here is a quick overview of the JN0-364 exam:

- Exam Type: 65 multiple-choice questions
- Exam Duration: 90 minutes
- Software Version: Junos OS 25.2
- Recommended Knowledge Level: Beginner to intermediate routing and switching experience with Junos

The exam verifies your understanding of routing and switching technologies and your ability to configure and troubleshoot related features in a Junos environment.

## What Topics Are Covered in the JN0-364 Exam?

The JN0-364 exam covers a broad range of important service provider networking technologies. Candidates should prepare for questions related to the following areas:

**Protocol-Independent Routing**

You should understand fundamental routing concepts that apply across different routing protocols. This includes route selection, routing tables, routing policies, and general routing operations within Junos OS.

**Open Shortest Path First (OSPF)**

OSPF is an important topic for JN0-364 candidates. Practice questions can help you review neighbor relationships, areas, LSAs, route calculation, configuration, and troubleshooting.

**Intermediate System to Intermediate System (IS-IS)**

Candidates should understand IS-IS operation and configuration in service provider networks. Focus on concepts such as levels, adjacencies, link-state databases, and route propagation.

**Border Gateway Protocol (BGP)**

BGP is a major routing technology in service provider environments. You should be prepared to answer questions about BGP peer relationships, route attributes, route selection, policies, and troubleshooting.

**Layer 2 Bridging and VLANs**

The exam also covers Layer 2 networking concepts, including Ethernet switching, VLAN configuration, bridging behavior, and related Junos configuration tasks.

**Spanning-Tree Protocols**

Candidates should understand the purpose and operation of spanning-tree technologies and how they help prevent Layer 2 loops.

**Multiprotocol Label Switching (MPLS)**

MPLS is a key technology for service provider networking. JN0-364 preparation should include MPLS fundamentals, label operations, and the interaction between MPLS and routing protocols.

**IPv6**

You should review IPv6 addressing, routing, protocol operation, and configuration concepts. Understanding how IPv6 is implemented alongside traditional networking technologies is important for the exam.

**Tunnels**

Tunnel technologies are another area included in the JN0-364 objectives. Candidates should understand basic tunnel concepts, operation, configuration, and applicable use cases.

**High Availability**

High availability is essential in modern networks. Prepare for questions involving redundancy, resilient network design, and technologies that help maintain service availability.

A structured preparation plan can help you study more efficiently.

1. Review the exam objectives. Start by organizing your study materials around the official JN0-364 topic areas.

2. Strengthen your Junos knowledge. Make sure you understand how networking technologies are configured and verified within Junos OS 25.2.

3. Study routing protocols carefully. Pay particular attention to OSPF, IS-IS, and BGP, including their operation, configuration, and troubleshooting.

4. Practice configuration scenarios. Reading concepts is useful, but hands-on practice can improve your ability to understand commands, outputs, and real-world troubleshooting situations.

5. Use JN0-364 practice questions. Practice questions with explanations can help you understand not only the correct answer but also why other options are incorrect.

6. Review your weak areas. Track the topics where you repeatedly make mistakes and spend additional study time improving those areas.

7. Take timed practice sessions. Since the exam contains 65 multiple-choice questions in 90 minutes, practicing under timed conditions can help improve exam readiness.
